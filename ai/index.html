AI buddy: what it is and what it is not

The AI buddy is an AI mentor for your daily commitment. It is software, not a person.

Not available yet. The AI buddy is part of Premium and is not in the current version of the app. This page describes how it will work.

If you are thinking about suicide or harming yourself: call your local emergency number now. In the United States, call or text 988. For other countries, find a helpline at findahelpline.com. Odos Areté is not a crisis service.

It is an AI

The AI buddy is an AI model, not a human. The app says so at the start of every conversation, and every reply is marked as AI-generated. If you are 15 to 17 years old, the app also reminds you every 3 hours that you are talking to an AI and suggests a break. Companion chatbots may not be suitable for some minors.

What it does

  • You tell it what you did and how, by speaking or typing. Speech is turned into text on your device.
  • It gives you a short retrospective, honest feedback and one concrete next step.
  • It speaks up on its own only in the situations you allowed in the questionnaire at the start, for example when something is unclear or when you are slipping. You can also choose chat only.
  • You choose the teachers it draws on: the Desert Fathers, ascetics, spiritual teachers from several religions, humanist thinkers, philosophers such as Nietzsche, or the Stoics.

Its voice

Its voice is written in our own words, from principles and a way of coaching that worked for the founder of Odos Areté: direct questions, one concrete step at a time, no flattery and no humiliation. It does not present itself as any real person. Apart from quotations from the teachers you chose, shown word for word with their source, it uses no one else’s texts.

How replies are made

  • On your device: where your device supports it, a model that runs on the device itself writes the reply (on iPhone and iPad, Apple’s on-device model), and what you write stays on your device.
  • Through our server: for paying subscribers, while our AI budget allows it, replies come from the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model through OpenRouter. The AI budget is funded only from revenue we have already received.
  • Written cards: where neither is possible, for example in a language the on-device model does not support, you get fixed written cards.

The Privacy Policy lists exactly what is sent in each case.

What it never sees

The AI buddy never receives your Screen Time data, the names of your apps, or anything about escaping into distractions. The app detects that on your device with fixed rules, not with AI. At most it lets the buddy start a conversation, without telling it what was detected.

Its limits

  • It can be wrong. Check anything important yourself.
  • It is not therapy, not medical or psychological advice, and not a crisis service. It does not diagnose or treat anything, and the app does not handle depression or suicidal thoughts.
  • If you write about suicide or self-harm, the app shows a card with the crisis lines above. The card does not change your mode, your commitment or app blocking. If you cannot keep up, use “I can’t keep up” in the app.

Report a reply

Every reply has a Report button. Reports come to us, and we use them to improve the buddy. You can also write to us at any time.
