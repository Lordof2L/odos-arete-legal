Frequently asked questions

What is free?

Everything in the current version is free: the daily intention and plan, focus sessions, habits, reflection, 30 days of proof, reminders, Apple Health readings and Focus protection. Premium features, such as the AI buddy, are planned as a subscription later.

How do I change my mode?

Go to More, Settings, Mode. You can change the mode 15 days after you chose it: pick the new mode and tap “Save mode”. Nightmare also needs you to accept its notice.

How do I set my reminder?

Go to More, Settings, Proof reminder. Choose the time and whether the notification shows neutral text, your direction or your latest proof. Choose neutral text if other people can see your lock screen.

How do I connect Apple Health?

Allow it in the intro, or go to More, Settings, Connect Apple Health. The app only reads data; it never writes to Apple Health, and the data stays on your device.

How does Focus protection work?

Go to More, Settings, Focus protection and allow Screen Time access. You then pick the apps and websites to block. The app cannot see which apps you picked or how you use them.

Is money involved?

Not in the current version. Your mode never depends on money.

How do I cancel a subscription or get a refund?

Subscriptions are sold through the App Store or Google Play, and the store handles cancellation and refunds. On iPhone, open Settings, tap your name, then Subscriptions. For a refund from Apple, use reportaproblem.apple.com.

How do I delete my data?

See Delete account.

I am in crisis.

Odos Areté is not a crisis service. If you are thinking about suicide or harming yourself, call your local emergency number now. In the United States, call or text 988. For other countries, find a helpline at findahelpline.com.
