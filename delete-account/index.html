Request deletion

The current version: no account

The current version of Odos Areté has no account, and all your data is on your device only. To delete it:

  1. Delete the app from your iPhone or iPad (touch and hold the app icon, then remove the app and confirm deletion).
  2. If you use Apple Watch, delete the watch app too.
  3. If you want, delete device backups that contain the app’s data.

We store none of your app data, so nothing else needs to be deleted. If you have written to us by email, you can ask us to delete that correspondence as well.

Once accounts exist

Money commitments will need an account. Once accounts exist, you can delete yours in two ways:

  • in the app: Settings, Delete account;
  • without the app: by email, as described at the top of this page.

Deleting your account does not cancel a Premium subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

What is deleted

  • Your account: user identifier, session and, after the last email about your amounts has been sent, your email address.
  • Your day records, time zone and all other commitment data, except what is listed below.
  • Your commitment ends for all future days.

What is kept, and for how long

The choice of payment provider is not final. This list describes the current plan with Stripe (Privacy Policy, section 10); if that changes, we update it before commitments start.

  • Records of agreements, charges and refunds are kept for 10 years from the end of the business year, as Swiss accounting law requires, in pseudonymous form: under a random account ID, without your Apple or Google identifier and without your email address.
  • A withdrawal statement you sent is kept with the name and email address you entered, for as long as we keep the records of its agreement; one that matches none of our contracts is deleted after 30 days.
  • Your email address stays on amounts that are still held or whose receipt we have not sent yet, until we have sent the last email about them.
  • An unsalted hash of your email address stays in our daily count of emails per address until our server, on a later day, first emails anyone a link or a confirmation (notices about holds and charges, and our own alerts, do not count).
  • Technical logs of our server at Cloudflare are kept for up to 7 days.
  • The alerts about holds and charges that our server sends us can remain in our database; they contain identifiers of your account, contract and charges, including those at Stripe, and dates and amounts, but not your email address.
  • Your card details, billing address and email address stay at Stripe until you ask us to delete them; we then delete or redact them as far as Stripe allows, and Stripe keeps what the law requires it to keep (see Stripe’s privacy policy).
  • If you asked us by email to delete your account, we keep your request and our reply as the record of the deletion.
  • Copies of the emails our server sent can remain in our Gmail mailboxes.
  • On your device, apps blocked after a missed day stay blocked until you record that day as done.

AI buddy data

AI buddy data on our server is not linked to an account; it is identified only by a salted hash of your subscription. You can delete it in the app’s AI settings or by email to us. Your conversations stored on your device are deleted with the app.
