Privacy Policy

This policy explains which personal data Odos Areté handles, where it stays, and which rights you have under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Part 1 describes the app as it is today. Part 2 describes the AI buddy and money commitments, which are not available yet.

In short

  • Today the app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. What you enter stays on your device, and we receive none of it.
  • Apple Health and Screen Time data are read on your device only and never leave it through the app.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone or calendar.
  • When the AI buddy and money commitments launch, some data will go to our server and to the services named in Part 2. The app asks you before any of that starts.

3. Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads from Apple Health: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate, sleep, and workouts with their distance and active energy. It shows them on the Body tab and on Apple Watch. From heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep it calculates a daily recovery state, which it stores on your device and shows in widgets if you add them. The app does not write to Apple Health. Health data is not sent to us or to anyone else, not used for advertising and not sold. You can change the app’s access in the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Health.

4. Screen Time (Focus protection)

If you turn on Focus protection, Apple’s Screen Time lets you pick apps, categories and websites to block, and apps and websites to allow. The app receives only anonymous tokens from Apple: it cannot see the names of the apps or websites you picked, or how you use them. It stores these tokens, whether protection is on, and the times you tapped the button on the block screen, on your device. None of this leaves your device. You can turn protection off in the app.

5. Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications that the app schedules on your device; we run no push server. What a reminder shows on your lock screen is your choice in the reminder settings. Widgets read the recovery state from your device.

6. What we receive

None of the data above. The app contains no analytics or advertising software and sends nothing to us; for the current version we run no server at all.

Apple handles the download of the app and any purchase as its own controller under its own privacy policy. If you allowed your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports; these do not tell us who you are.

If you write to us, we use your message and your email address only to answer you, based on our legitimate interest in answering (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and we delete them when they are no longer needed for that.

7. Backups and deletion

Deleting the app deletes the data it stored on your device; on Apple Watch, delete the watch app too. Backups of your device (in iCloud or on a computer) may keep a copy until the backup is replaced or deleted; you and Apple control those backups. Because we hold none of your app data, there is nothing for us to delete. See also Delete account.

9. AI buddy

The AI buddy replies in one of three ways (see AI buddy):

  • On your device. Where your device supports it, a model that runs on the device itself writes the reply (on iPhone and iPad, Apple’s on-device model). What you write stays on your device.
  • Through our server. For paying subscribers, while our AI budget allows it, the reply comes from the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model. The app then sends to our server, which runs on Cloudflare: what you typed (speech is turned into text on your device), your mode, your app language, today’s record, counts from the last 7 days, the teacher you chose, the situation that started the conversation (for example a missed plan or low evening energy) and your last few messages. Our server passes this to OpenRouter, which passes it to the company that runs the model. We configure these requests to use only providers that state they do not use them for training. The app shows the exact recipients and countries before you consent.
  • Written cards. Where neither is possible, you get fixed written cards, and nothing you write is sent.

Never sent: audio, app names, Screen Time data, and anything about escaping into distractions. That is detected on your device by fixed rules, not by the AI, and it stays there.

Counts without content: the app sends, per day, how often the crisis card was shown, as a number without any content or link to you. If you agree to share usage data during your first 30 days, it also sends which situations fired and whether you opened them, which situations you have turned on, how many replies came each way, daily totals of the “I can’t keep up” options used (not linked to you), and the ratings and notes you choose to send.

What we keep: no message text, except messages you report and feedback you send us; the counts above; and daily usage and cost counters for the server route. The server route identifies you only by a salted hash of your App Store or Google Play subscription, not by your name or email. We keep reports and feedback until we have dealt with them, and you can have them deleted at any time. We keep usage and cost counters only as long as we need them to enforce the daily limit and to account for our costs. We keep the usage counts you shared from your first 30 days only as long as we need them to evaluate and improve the buddy. Daily crisis-card counts are not linked to anyone; we keep them as long as we need them for reports the law requires. On your device the app keeps your conversation for 30 days.

Legal basis: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a), because a conversation can touch on your health). You can withdraw it at any time in the app’s AI settings; everything else keeps working. Where the law of your country sets a higher age for this consent than your age, the AI buddy stays off until a parent or guardian agrees or you reach that age.

10. Money commitments

Account. For a commitment you sign in with Apple (or with Google on Android). We receive a user identifier and your email address (Apple may give us a relay address instead) and keep a session token.

What we process: your day records (done or not done, time, type and class of proof, a sequence number); your time zone; your agreement (amount, currency, recipient, billing country, version of the terms, time of acceptance, IP address and browser, your age confirmation); a card reference from Stripe (customer and payment-method identifiers, card brand, last four digits and a card fingerprint, never the full card number); holds, charges, refunds and disputes; withdrawal statements; and the emails we send you.

Who else receives data:

  • Stripe processes your card and the payments. You enter your card on Stripe’s page, and Stripe’s own privacy policy also applies.
  • A charity or person you chose as recipient receives the amount through Stripe. We do not give them your name or email address.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the emails we send you.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database.

How long: records of agreements, charges and refunds are kept for 10 years from the end of the business year, as Swiss accounting law requires (Art. 958f of the Code of Obligations). After you delete your account they are kept only in pseudonymous form, without your identifier, email or IP address. Your email address is deleted after the last receipt has been sent. Everything else is deleted when you delete your account.

Legal basis: the contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)); our legal obligation to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c)); and our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and defending legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f)).

Screen Time data and escape data never leave your device, also when you have a commitment.

11. Transfers abroad

Cloudflare, Stripe, OpenRouter, the company that runs the model and Google may process data in the United States and in other countries. Where a country does not have a level of data protection recognised as adequate by Switzerland or the EU, we rely on the safeguards the law provides, such as standard contractual clauses or the recipient’s certification under the Data Privacy Framework.

12. Your rights

Under the FADP and the GDPR you can ask for access to your data, correction, deletion, restriction, a copy in a portable format, and you can object to processing and withdraw consent at any time. Write to lukasllitvak@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. Today your app data is only on your device, where you can view, change and delete it yourself.

You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch) or, if you live in the EU or EEA, to the data protection authority of your country.
