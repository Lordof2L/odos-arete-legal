Privacy Policy

This policy explains which personal data Odos Areté handles, where it stays, and which rights you have under the Swiss Federal Act on Data Protection (FADP) and the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). Part 1 describes the app as it is today. Part 2 describes the AI buddy and money commitments, which are not available yet.

In short

  • Today the app has no account, no server, no analytics and no advertising. What you enter stays on your device, and we receive none of it.
  • Apple Health and Screen Time data are read on your device only and never leave it through the app.
  • The app does not use your location, contacts, camera, photos, microphone or calendar.
  • When the AI buddy and money commitments launch, some data will go to our server and to the services named in Part 2. The app asks you before any of that starts.

3. Apple Health

If you allow it, the app reads from Apple Health: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, heart rate, sleep, and workouts with their distance and active energy. It shows them on the Body tab and on Apple Watch. From heart rate variability, resting heart rate and sleep it calculates a daily recovery state, which it stores on your device and shows in widgets if you add them. The app does not write to Apple Health. Health data is not sent to us or to anyone else, not used for advertising and not sold. You can change the app’s access in the iOS Settings under Privacy & Security, Health.

4. Screen Time (Focus protection)

If you turn on Focus protection, Apple’s Screen Time lets you pick apps, categories and websites to block, and apps and websites to allow. The app receives only anonymous tokens from Apple: it cannot see the names of the apps or websites you picked, or how you use them. It stores these tokens, whether protection is on, and the times you tapped the button on the block screen, on your device. None of this leaves your device. You can turn protection off in the app.

5. Reminders and widgets

Reminders are local notifications that the app schedules on your device; we run no push server. What a reminder shows on your lock screen is your choice in the reminder settings. Widgets read the recovery state from your device.

6. What we receive

None of the data above. The app contains no analytics or advertising software and sends nothing to us; for the current version we run no server at all.

Apple handles the download of the app and any purchase as its own controller under its own privacy policy. If you allowed your device to share analytics with app developers, Apple may give us aggregated usage statistics and crash reports; these do not tell us who you are.

If you write to us, we use your message and your email address only to answer you, based on our legitimate interest in answering (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)), and we delete them when they are no longer needed for that. A request to delete your account is an exception: we keep it, with our reply, as the record of the deletion (section 10).

7. Backups and deletion

Deleting the app deletes the data it stored on your device; on Apple Watch, delete the watch app too. Backups of your device (in iCloud or on a computer) may keep a copy until the backup is replaced or deleted; you and Apple control those backups. Because we hold none of your app data, there is nothing for us to delete. See also Delete account.

8. Children

Odos Areté is not meant for anyone under 15.

9. AI buddy

The AI buddy replies in one of three ways (see AI buddy):

  • On your device. Where your device supports it, a model that runs on the device itself writes the reply (on iPhone and iPad, Apple’s on-device model). What you write stays on your device.
  • Through our server. For paying subscribers, while our AI budget allows it, the reply comes from the DeepSeek V4.1 Flash model. The app then sends to our server, which runs on Cloudflare: what you typed (speech is turned into text on your device), your mode, your app language, today’s record, counts from the last 7 days, the teacher you chose, the situation that started the conversation (for example a missed plan or low evening energy) and your last few messages. Our server passes this to OpenRouter, which passes it to the company that runs the model. We configure these requests to use only providers that state they do not use them for training. The app shows the exact recipients and countries before you consent.
  • Written cards. Where neither is possible, you get fixed written cards, and nothing you write is sent.

Never sent: audio, app names, Screen Time data, and anything about escaping into distractions. That is detected on your device by fixed rules, not by the AI, and it stays there.

Counts without content: the app sends, per day, how often the crisis card was shown, as a number without any content or link to you. If you agree to share usage data during your first 30 days, it also sends which situations fired and whether you opened them, which situations you have turned on, how many replies came each way, daily totals of the “I can’t keep up” options used (not linked to you), and the ratings and notes you choose to send.

What we keep: no message text, except messages you report and feedback you send us; the counts above; and daily usage and cost counters for the server route. The server route identifies you only by a salted hash of your App Store or Google Play subscription, not by your name or email. We keep reports and feedback until we have dealt with them, and you can have them deleted at any time. We keep usage and cost counters only as long as we need them to enforce the daily limit and to account for our costs. We keep the usage counts you shared from your first 30 days only as long as we need them to evaluate and improve the buddy. Daily crisis-card counts are not linked to anyone; we keep them as long as we need them for reports the law requires. On your device the app keeps your conversation for 30 days.

Legal basis: your explicit consent (GDPR Art. 6(1)(a) and Art. 9(2)(a), because a conversation can touch on your health). You can withdraw it at any time in the app’s AI settings; everything else keeps working. Where the law of your country sets a higher age for this consent than your age, the AI buddy stays off until a parent or guardian agrees or you reach that age.

10. Money commitments

The choice of payment provider is not final. This section describes the current plan with Stripe; if that changes, we update this section before commitments start.

Account. For a commitment you sign in with Apple (or with Google on Android). We receive a user identifier and your email address (Apple may give us a relay address instead; from Google we take it only if Google has verified it). You need an email address for a commitment, because we send the setup link and every notice about a hold or a charge to it. When you sign in, the app also sends your age group (15, 16 to 17, or 18 and over), your mode (and whether you use Easy) and your time zone; from them we decide which modes and commitments are open to you, how many missed days you can make up and when each of your days ends. We keep a hash of your session token and, if you sign in with Apple, an encrypted Apple token that lets us end Sign in with Apple for Odos Areté when you delete your account.

What we process: your day records (done or not done, time, type and class of proof, a sequence number); your time zone and its changes; when your account was created, the day from which we count your days, and when you last changed your mode (for the 15-day mode lock); your agreement (the currency and the amount per missed day, the version and language of the terms, the sentences you ticked, including that you are 18 or older, when you confirmed, and the ID of the Stripe page where you then entered your card, by which we match your confirmation to that card); your billing address, of which our server keeps only the country, which decides whether our withdrawal page applies to your contract (with an address in the EU or EEA); a card reference from Stripe (customer and payment-method identifiers, the last four digits, which we show you with your commitment, and a card fingerprint, with which we refuse a new commitment with the same card within 30 days of a withdrawal; never the full card number); holds, charges, declines, refunds, and any dispute or fraud warning about a charge; a withdrawal statement, if you send one through our withdrawal page (the name, email address and contract ID you enter, the time and the outcome); and the emails we send you.

Our server uses your IP address only to limit how many sign-ins, deletion requests and withdrawal statements can come from the same internet connection per minute; it does not save it in our database. Cloudflare may keep technical logs of our server for up to 7 days. They can contain your IP address and an approximate location derived from it, details of your browser or app, the web addresses you or the app open on our server (the withdrawal link in our email that confirms your commitment contains your email address) and identifiers of your account, contract and charges, including those at Stripe.

Who else receives data:

  • Stripe processes your card and the payments. You enter your card details, billing address and email address on Stripe’s page; Stripe keeps them in our Stripe account, and Stripe’s own privacy policy also applies. If you dispute a charge with your card issuer, we may give the issuer, through Stripe, the records of that charge and of your agreement.
  • Google (Gmail) delivers the emails we send you. If you sign in with Google, Google confirms your sign-in.
  • Apple confirms your sign-in if you sign in with Apple, and forwards our emails to you if you use a relay address.
  • Cloudflare hosts our server and database.

Charged amounts go only to us (section 1); no charity or other person receives them.

Holds and charges follow automatically from your day records, under the rules you confirmed when you set up your commitment. If you disagree with a hold or a charge, you can contest it by writing to us (section 12), and Lukáš Litvák reviews your objection himself; you can also dispute a charge with your card issuer.

How long: records of agreements, charges and refunds are kept for 10 years from the end of the business year, as Swiss accounting law requires (Art. 958f of the Code of Obligations). Your account and your sign-in with us (including the hashes of sessions you signed out of), your day records, your time-zone history and the records of the links we emailed you are kept until you delete your account, and then deleted. What stays after the deletion:

  • the records of your agreements, charges and refunds, under a random account ID, without your Apple or Google identifier and without your email address;
  • a withdrawal statement you sent, with the name and email address you entered, as evidence of what we received, for as long as we keep the records of its agreement; a statement that matches none of our contracts is deleted after 30 days, but a copy of our alert email about it, with the same details, can remain (see the last point);
  • your email address on amounts that are still held or whose receipt we have not sent yet, until we have sent the last email about them;
  • an unsalted hash of your email address in our daily count of emails per address, until our server, on a later day, first emails anyone a link or a confirmation (notices about holds and charges, and our own alerts, do not count);
  • technical logs of our server at Cloudflare, for up to 7 days;
  • the alerts about holds and charges that our server sends us (with identifiers of your account, contract and charges, including those at Stripe, and with dates and amounts, but without your email address), which can remain in our database;
  • your card details, billing address and email address at Stripe, until you ask us to delete them; we then delete or redact them as far as Stripe allows, and Stripe keeps what the law requires it to keep (see Stripe’s privacy policy);
  • your request by email to delete your account, if you sent one, and our reply, which we keep as the record of the deletion;
  • copies of the emails our server sent to you and to us, which can remain in our Gmail mailboxes.

Legal basis:

  • the contract with you (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)): your account, day records, agreement, card, holds, charges, declines, refunds and withdrawal, and the emails about them;
  • our legal obligation to keep accounting records (Art. 6(1)(c)): the records kept for 10 years;
  • our legitimate interest in preventing abuse and keeping our server running (Art. 6(1)(f)): the limit on requests per internet connection, the daily count of emails per address, the card fingerprint, the technical logs and the alerts about holds and charges that our server sends us;
  • our legitimate interest in proving what happened and defending legal claims (Art. 6(1)(f)): disputes and fraud warnings about a charge, withdrawal statements, a request by email to delete your account and our reply, copies of our emails, and your data at Stripe after you delete your account.

Screen Time data and escape data never leave your device, also when you have a commitment.

12. Your rights

Write to lukasllitvak@gmail.com; we answer within 30 days. Today your app data is only on your device, where you can view, change and delete it yourself.

You can also complain to the Swiss Federal Data Protection and Information Commissioner (edoeb.admin.ch) or, if you live in the EU or EEA, to the data protection authority of your country.
